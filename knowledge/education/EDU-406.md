# Keeping Your Contact Details Up to Date

Program messages reach you only if your contact details are current.

## Checklist
- Your phone number and email address.
- Your preferred language.
- Your preferred channel: App, SMS or Email.

To update any of these, contact program staff. They change your profile for you.
