# Understanding Your Care Plan Tasks

Your care plan is a list of tasks set up by your care team. Each task has a name, how often it repeats, a due date and a status.

## Statuses
- Due or Overdue: the task needs your attention.
- Open or Scheduled: the task is coming up.
- Completed: the task is done.

The status is shown exactly as recorded by your care team.
