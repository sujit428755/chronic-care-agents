# Preparing for a Care Call

A short checklist to help you get the most from a call with your care coordinator.

## Before the call
- Check that your recent readings are entered in the app.
- Write down your questions so you do not forget them during the call.
- Look at your task list and note anything you could not finish.
- Find a quiet place and keep your phone charged.

## During the call
- Ask the coordinator to repeat anything that is unclear.
- Note down any follow-up dates you agree on.

## After the call
- Check the app for any new tasks or dates the coordinator added.

This checklist is general guidance and not medical advice.
