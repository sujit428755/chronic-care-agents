# Questions to Note Before a Clinic Visit

A short written list helps you use your clinic visit well.

## Ideas
- Write down questions as they come to mind.
- Bring your device or your list of entries.
- Note anything you want your clinician to explain in their own words.

The program does not answer clinical questions. Your clinician does.
