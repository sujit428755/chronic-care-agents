# Using the Reading Log in the App

The reading log is the place in the app where your entries are kept in date order.

## Adding an entry
- Choose the type of entry, such as glucose, blood pressure or weight.
- Type the number exactly as your device shows it.
- Check the date and time before you save.

## Fixing a mistake
If you typed the wrong number, enter the correct one and tell program staff. The old entry is marked as replaced so only the corrected number is used.
