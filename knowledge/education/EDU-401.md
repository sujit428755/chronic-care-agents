# Understanding Home Readings

A home reading is a measurement you take yourself, for example a glucose reading, a blood pressure reading or your weight, and then record in the program app.

## Why the program asks you to record readings
- Regular entries give your care team a clear record to look at together with you.
- Recording the date and time with every reading helps your care coordinator see when each reading was taken.
- If you miss a day, simply record the next reading. Do not guess or fill in a value for a day you did not measure.

## Tips for recording readings
- Record each reading as soon as you take it.
- Use the same device each time when possible.
- If you entered something by mistake, you can correct the entry. The corrected value replaces the old one in your active record.

## What this article does not do
This article is general education only. It does not tell you what a particular reading means for you, and it is not medical advice. Your care team is the right place to ask what your readings mean for your own health.
