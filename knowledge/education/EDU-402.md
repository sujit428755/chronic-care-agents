# Building a Daily Routine

Small, repeatable habits make it easier to keep up with your program tasks.

## Habit ideas
- Attach recording your reading to something you already do every day, such as your morning routine.
- Keep your meter and your phone in the same place so both are easy to find.
- Turn on the program reminders so the app can nudge you at your chosen time.
- Tick off each task in the app when it is done so you can see your progress.

## If you miss a day
Missing a day happens. Start again the next day and let your care coordinator know if you are finding the routine hard to keep.

## Please note
This article is for habit support only. It does not change your treatment plan. Any question about your medicines or treatment should go to your doctor or care team.
