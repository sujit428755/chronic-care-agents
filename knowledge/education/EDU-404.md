# When the Program Contacts You

Program staff may contact you in a few situations.

## Situations where staff may reach out
- Your consent for the program has expired and needs to be renewed.
- An entry you recorded matches a rule that the program has set up for staff review.
- You asked a question that the app cannot answer, or asked for something only a clinician can decide.
- Information in your record looks inconsistent and a person needs to check it.

## How you will be contacted
Staff use the contact channel saved in your program profile (App, SMS or Email) and your preferred language where possible.

## Important
- When the program flags an entry for staff review, this is a routine process step. It is not a statement about what the entry means.
- The app and this assistant do not give emergency care. If you need urgent help, contact your care team or local emergency services.
