# Getting Started with Hypertension Support

Welcome to Hypertension Support. This program helps you stay in touch with your care team.

## What to expect
- Reminders for the tasks in your care plan.
- A place to enter your blood pressure entries.
- Staff follow-up when something needs a person to look at it.

Questions about your treatment are for your clinician.
