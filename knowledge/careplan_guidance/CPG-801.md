# How care plan tasks and statuses work

Each task in your care plan has a name, a frequency (Daily, Monthly, Once), a next due date and a status.

- Due: the task is ready for you to do.
- Overdue: the due date has passed and the task is not yet marked done.
- Open: the task is available and has a due date in the future.
- Scheduled: the task is planned for a future date, such as a coordinator check-in.

The statuses come from your care plan record. The assistant reports them as recorded and does not change them.
