# What happens to the readings I enter?

Readings you record are stored in your program record and can be seen by your care coordinator. The assistant can describe your entries, such as how many you recorded and which days have no entry, but it does not say what a reading means. If you correct an entry, the corrected value replaces the earlier one in your active record.
